P4_flats - twilight flat fields for 12.410 Problem Set 4
========================================================

The camera is the CMOS imager on the Wallace Shed C14 (Pier 3), at exactly
the settings of Problem Set 3: binned 2x2, HCG mode, gain slider 100, offset
30, sensor at -10 C.  There are no bias or dark frames here, because you
already have them: your P3 master bias, dark-rate map and bad-pixel mask are
the calibration for these frames too.

    twilight/        10 r' twilight flats, evening of 2026 Oct 3, taken one
                     after another as the sky faded, the telescope nudged
                     between each one

The plate scale is 0.397 arcsec per pixel.
