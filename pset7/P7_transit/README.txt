P7_transit - one transit of HAT-P-32 b for 12.410 Problem Set 7
===============================================================

HAT-P-32, observed through the r' filter on the Wallace Shed C14
(Pier 3) on the night of 2025 Oct 9-10 by last year's class, with
the telescope slightly defocused.  This is comparison star C1 from
Problem Set 6, observed on a different night.  The frames have been
reduced, aperture photometry has been done, and HAT-P-32's flux has
been divided by the summed flux of six comparison stars in the same
field.  What is here is that relative light curve, one row per 45 s
exposure, normalized so that its median outside the transit is 1.

    HAT-P-32_2025-10-10.csv

Columns:

    date_obs     start of the exposure, UTC
    bjd_tdb      mid-exposure time, BJD_TDB
    airmass      airmass at mid-exposure
    flux         relative flux of HAT-P-32
    err          its uncertainty: Poisson noise of the stars and the
                 sky, plus scintillation, for HAT-P-32 and the
                 comparison stars together

Measure time in hours from BJD_TDB 2460958.62 (02:51 UTC), the middle
of the night.  This table is all the set needs: nothing from Problem
Set 6 is required.
